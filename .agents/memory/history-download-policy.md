---
name: History download policy
description: User-approved cloud history retention and plan-specific download windows
---
Upload all sales history to Supabase. Basic accounts download two calendar months of new history; Premium-approved accounts download two years. Do not delete older cloud history or older records already saved locally. Keep the current catalog, unpaid bills and recent repayments available regardless of the original invoice date.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “Upload all history, limit downloads,” rather than a policy that stops uploading older history. A download cutoff must not erase records or prevent collecting older credit.

**How to apply:** Filter new downloads rather than pruning stored data. Reconcile previously saved historical bills without loading an entire archive onto a fresh device. Preserve optimistic revisions, offline changes and conflict handling. Premium's storage allowance follows approval, not a temporary choice to use the simpler Basic interface.
